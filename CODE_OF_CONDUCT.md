# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project.

Participants must communicate constructively, respect differing experience and
backgrounds, accept technical feedback in good faith, and avoid harassment,
personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, or publication of private
information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should
consider context, impact, repetition, and opportunities to correct behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner profile. Do not
include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and in good faith.
